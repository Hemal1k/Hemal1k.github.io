HeMiTube

A browser extension that makes YouTube calmer and more comfortable: a real theater mode, the dislike count back, the quality and codecs you want – and more you can turn on when you like, such as hiding Shorts.

Download .zip GitHub

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and other Chromium browsers. Not in the Chrome Web Store yet, so the browser loads it as an unpacked extension:

  1. Unpack HeMiTube.zip into a new, empty folder that stays (not one you clean up, like Downloads).
  2. Open the extensions page of the browser: chrome://extensions, edge://extensions, brave://extensions, opera://extensions or vivaldi://extensions.
  3. Turn on Developer mode – top right; in Edge in the panel on the left (under ☰ in a narrow window).
  4. Click Load unpacked and choose the folder.

After an update – by hand or by HeMi Launcher – click ⟳ at HeMiTube on the extensions page and reload YouTube. To check a download, run Get-FileHash HeMiTube.zip in PowerShell – the hash must be the same as in SHA256SUMS.txt of the release. HeMi Launcher unpacks it, opens a short guide in your browser (Add to browser) and puts updates in place.

What it does

Theater mode

The player across the whole page, the top bar hidden until the mouse reaches it – and, if you switch it on, started by itself for every video; the ambient glow and the live chat optional.

Dislikes back

The number of dislikes next to the like button, also on Shorts, optionally a like / dislike bar – from Return YouTube Dislike, read only.

Quality

By default the smallest quality that fills your screen, or a fixed one; a 30 FPS limit; its own quality for videos on other websites.

Codecs

Which video (AV1, VP9, H.264) and audio codecs YouTube may use and in what order – for older or weaker hardware.

Hide Shorts

Switch it on and Shorts are gone from the home page, subscriptions, search and recommendations; links to Shorts can also open in the normal player.

When you want more

A default speed, skipping sponsors (SponsorBlock), hiding end screens, cards, recommendations or comments, a loop A–B, a screenshot of the video, shortcuts – all off until you turn them on.
