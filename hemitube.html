HeMiTube

A browser extension that makes YouTube calmer and more comfortable: a real theatre mode, the dislike count back, the quality and codecs you want, and no Shorts – with more you can turn on when you like.

Download .zip GitHub

Chrome, Edge, Brave, Opera, Vivaldi and other Chromium browsers. Not in the Chrome Web Store yet: unpack the zip, open the extensions page of the browser, turn on Developer mode and Load unpacked the folder. HeMi Launcher unpacks it, opens a short guide in your browser (Add to browser) and keeps it up to date.

What it does

Theatre mode

The player across the whole page, the top bar hidden until the mouse reaches it – turned on by itself for every video; the ambient glow and the live chat optional.

Dislikes back

The number of dislikes next to the like button, also on Shorts, optionally a like / dislike bar – from Return YouTube Dislike, read only.

Quality

By default the smallest quality that fills your screen, or a fixed one; a 30 FPS limit; its own quality for videos on other websites.

Codecs

Which video (AV1, VP9, H.264) and audio codecs YouTube may use and in what order – for older or weaker hardware.

No Shorts

Shorts gone from the home page, subscriptions, search and recommendations; links to Shorts open in the normal player.

When you want more

A default speed, skipping sponsors (SponsorBlock), hiding end screens, cards, recommendations or comments, a loop A–B, a screenshot of the video, shortcuts – all off until you turn them on.
