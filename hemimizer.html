How it works

1. Before reinstalling

Start HeMimizer on the old PC: Back up this PC. The wizard asks what the new Windows gets, and HeMimizer saves it with your settings – and, if you like, all installers – into a folder of your choice, a USB stick or a network drive. It copies itself there too.

2. After reinstalling

Start HeMimizer from that folder: Set up this PC. Drivers first, then the programs, the settings of the old PC and the clean-up of Windows – it goes on by itself after restarts.

No backup?

Set up this PC runs the same wizard on the new PC: choose drivers, a browser, programs and the changes of Windows, and HeMimizer does the rest.

The wizard

Drivers by the hardware

Ticked by what it finds: NVIDIA graphics, AMD chipset and Radeon graphics, Intel drivers; the tool of the maker of a laptop (HP, Lenovo, Dell, ASUS, Acer, MSI) or of the motherboard of a PC built from parts.

About 40 programs

A browser besides Edge, and programs in sections – 7-Zip, VLC, Steam, Discord, Spotify, LibreOffice, VS Code, Git, Python, Java… – the newest versions through winget.

Licence terms

Programs with their own terms are listed with a link to them, and HeMimizer installs them only after you accept.

A cleaner Windows

A restore point first; Copilot, ads and tips off; the apps of Microsoft few people use and the trial software of makers removed – each one a switch.

Your settings along

Wallpaper, colours, pinned apps, File Explorer, Defender, network drives, display scaling and the settings of VLC, qBittorrent, Brave, Notepad and AMD Software – plus folders of your own.

Offline, if you like

The backup can download every installer at once: the new PC then installs everything before it is even connected to the internet.
