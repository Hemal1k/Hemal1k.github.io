How it works

1. Before reinstalling

Start HeMimizer on the old PC: Back up this PC. The wizard asks what the new Windows gets, and HeMimizer saves it with your settings – and, if you like, all installers – into a folder of your choice, a USB stick or a network drive. It copies itself there too.

2. After reinstalling

Start HeMimizer from that folder: Set up this PC. Drivers first, then the programs, the settings of the old PC and the clean-up of Windows – it goes on by itself after restarts.

No backup?

Set up this PC runs the same wizard on the new PC: choose drivers, a browser, programs and the changes of Windows, and HeMimizer does the rest.

The wizard

Drivers by the hardware

Ticked by what it finds: NVIDIA graphics, AMD chipset and Radeon graphics, Intel drivers; the tool of the maker of a laptop (HP, Lenovo, Dell, ASUS, Acer, MSI) or of the motherboard of a PC built from parts.

About 60 programs

A browser besides Edge, and programs in sections with a search – 7-Zip, VLC, Steam, Epic, Ubisoft, EA, Discord, Spotify, LibreOffice, VS Code, the free creative apps of ArtCraft… – the newest versions through winget.

Licence terms

At the setup, programs with their own terms are listed with a link to them – HeMimizer installs them only after you accept, or leaves them out.

Windows your way

Over 30 changes, each a switch: a restore point, Copilot, ads and tips off, the apps of Microsoft few people use removed, the taskbar, Start and File Explorer – recommended, or set like your old PC.

Your settings along

Wallpaper, colours, pinned apps, File Explorer, the mouse, Defender, mapped network drives, display scaling and the settings of your programs – VLC, Brave, Discord, OBS, VS Code with its extensions and more, also Adobe apps, DaVinci Resolve or Blender found on the PC – plus folders of your own.

Shortcuts your way

For each new program: a shortcut on the desktop, pinned to Start or the taskbar, started with Windows – only what you tick. HeMimizer does nothing you did not choose.

Offline, if you like

The backup can download every installer at once – and pack everything into one .zip that is easy to move. The new PC then installs everything before it is even connected to the internet.

Protected with a password

Your settings, Wi-Fi passwords and folders encrypted (AES-256) – HeMimizer asks for the password on the new PC. At the end it removes itself: no log, nothing of it stays.

Tools

A Windows USB stick

From a Windows 10 or 11 ISO, like Rufus: GPT or MBR, and your choices for the installation – no TPM check, no Microsoft account required, a local account, the regional settings of this PC, no privacy questions, no BitLocker.

A smaller Windows

A lighter Windows 11 ISO in the spirit of Tiny11, by HeMimizer's own list: without ads, Teams, Xbox, OneDrive and other extras – the simple choice, or choose yourself what goes and what stays.
